Our Process

End-to-End Standardized Process

Whether it's traditional cross-border trade or emerging DTC e-commerce expansion, we have prepared a transparent and efficient delivery path for you.

🚢 International Trade (B2B)

From Source Factory to Overseas Buyers

🛒 Cross-Border E-Commerce (DTC)

From Store Setup to Global Brand Expansion

Step 1

Sourcing & Product Selection

Based on overseas market demand, we precisely select high-quality domestic source factories for product sampling and preliminary quotation evaluation.

Step 1

Store Setup & Compliance

Assisting with account registration and brand registry on platforms like Amazon and Shopee, as well as the basic setup of DTC websites (Shopify).

Step 2

Mass Production & QC

After order confirmation, production is scheduled. Our professional team conducts mid-term and final quality control (QC) to ensure products meet international standards.

Step 2

Localized Selection & Listing Optimization

Using big data to analyze market trends and determine hero products. We design high-conversion detail pages, layout keywords, and optimize A+ content.

Step 3

Customs Clearance & Logistics

Preparing full sets of export documents, arranging sea/air freight and compliant customs clearance. We provide real-time tracking to ensure safe delivery.

Step 3

Ad Placement & Traffic Generation

Formulating PPC advertising strategies, combined with off-site Deal sites, KOL reviews, and social media matrices to fully ignite store traffic.

Step 4

Overseas Warehousing & B2B Delivery

Assisting in allocating goods to overseas warehouses (FBA or third-party) or directly delivering them to overseas buyers, completing the trade loop.

Step 4

TikTok Live & Social Media Conversion

Setting up overseas live streaming rooms and connecting with local KOLs for sales. Fine-tuning private domain traffic operations to achieve high repurchase rates.

Step 5

Data Review & Brand Upgrade

Monthly operational data review and marketing strategy adjustment. Transitioning from white-label OEM to DTC brand building to establish brand premium.
