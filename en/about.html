FUNSTOCK LIMITED

FUNSTOCK LIMITED is a dynamic and innovative international trading and technical service company. Headquartered in Tuen Mun Industrial Centre, New Territories, Hong Kong, we leverage Hong Kong's advantages as an international logistics and financial hub to efficiently integrate the manufacturing supply chain of the Greater China region.

Our business spans technical consulting, international import and export trade, cross-border e-commerce, and marketing planning. We are committed to helping global clients discover high-quality Chinese smart manufacturing products, providing end-to-end services from product selection and technical support to digital marketing.
