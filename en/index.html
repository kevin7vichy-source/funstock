About Funstock

Based in Hong Kong, Empowering a New Global Trade Ecosystem

趣倉有限公司FUNSTOCK LIMITED

FUNSTOCK LIMITED (趣倉有限公司) is a dynamic and innovative international trading and technical services company. Headquartered at Tuen Mun Industrial Centre, New Territories, Hong Kong, it leverages Hong Kong's strengths as an international logistics and financial hub to efficiently integrate manufacturing supply chains across Greater China.

Our business spans technical consulting, international import and export trade, cross-border e-commerce and marketing planning. We help global customers discover quality China-made products, providing end-to-end services from product selection and technical support to digital marketing.
